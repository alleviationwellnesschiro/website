About

Dr. Pasqueal Bigoms, Douglasville chiropractor

A caring, intuitive doctor—focused on neurological chiropractic for the whole family.

Doctor of Chiropractic

Proudly serving Douglasville & Douglas County

Dr. Pasqueal Bigoms is a Georgia native. Raised in Decatur, he graduated from Life University with dual degrees in nutrition and chiropractic. He then worked in offices throughout the Atlanta area, mastering techniques including full spine, Torque Release Technique, and atlas orthogonal.

Dr. Bigoms is excited to give back to the community he calls home and looks forward to spreading health and wellness across Douglas County. He is an avid football fan (Go Falcons!), and you can catch him around town with his wife, Shavon, and sons PJ and Miles.
