Extremity care

Hand & foot chiropractor in Douglasville

Specialized care for hands, wrists, feet, and ankles—alongside the spine—so you can work, play, and move with less pain.

Why extremities matter

More than a “back crack” clinic

Your hands and feet carry the load of daily life—typing, lifting, walking, training. When joints in the extremities aren’t moving well, pain and numbness can show up even when your spine feels fine.

At Alleviation Wellness, Dr. Bigoms evaluates and adjusts the extremities as part of whole-body neurological care. Whether you’re dealing with desk-related wrist pain or heel pain that makes every step frustrating, we’ll help you understand what’s going on and build a clear plan.

Conditions we help with

Hand, foot & related joint concerns

Our experienced team is trained to help relieve pain and support better function. Common reasons patients seek extremity care include:

Hand & wrist pain

Specialized care for carpal tunnel–type symptoms, wrist stiffness, and hand injuries to support function and comfort.

Foot & ankle pain

Support for plantar fasciitis, ankle sprains, and other foot-related issues that limit walking or standing.

Shoulder pain

Non-invasive techniques to improve range of motion and ease stiffness after overuse or injury.

Elbow pain

Targeted care for tennis elbow, golfer’s elbow, and related joint irritation.

Spine pain

Comprehensive spinal care for neck and back pain that often travels with extremity issues.

Auto & work injuries

Care for soft-tissue and joint injuries after collisions or workplace strain—documented when needed for claims.

What to expect

Your first extremity visit

We’ll listen to your history, examine how your joints move, and explain findings in plain language. Care may include specific extremity adjustments, spinal care when indicated, and guidance on activity so healing can stick.

Many patients are surprised how much hand or foot comfort improves when those joints are addressed directly—not only when the neck or low back is adjusted.

When to come in

Don’t wait on numbness or heel pain

Seek an evaluation if you notice:

  • Tingling or numbness in the hands or fingers
  • Morning heel pain or plantar fascia discomfort
  • Grip weakness or wrist stiffness at work
  • Ankle instability after a sprain
  • Shoulder or elbow pain that limits lifting or sports

Not sure if chiropractic extremity care is right for you? Contact us—we’re happy to help you decide next steps.
