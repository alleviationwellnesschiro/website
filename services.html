Services

Chiropractic services in Douglasville

Gold-standard wellness care and therapies—tailored to get you out of pain, restore function, and help you feel your best.

Torque Release Technique

Dr. Bigoms has Certified Advanced Proficiency in Torque Release Technique (TRT)—a specific, reproducible, gentle approach that does not involve twisting, popping, or cracking of the spine. Practice members of any age can experience meaningful results. Research published in journals including Nature and Molecular Psychiatry has associated TRT care with improvements in measures of well-being.

Children’s Care

Our motto: “We’d rather raise healthy kids than fix sick adults.” We see children from a few days old through the teenage years. Families often seek care for concerns such as colic, ear infections, ADHD-related challenges, allergies, bedwetting, sleep issues, and acid reflux—as a drug-free support alongside their healthcare team.

Traumatic Events & Auto Injury

After an accident, soft-tissue and spinal injuries can linger. We combine chiropractic care with in-office therapies to help restore function. We can document care for attorneys or insurance adjusters when needed.

Class IV Laser

Class IV lasers help stimulate cellular energy (ATP), which can support faster healing, reduced pain and swelling, and improved range of motion—especially helpful alongside hand and foot care. Learn more about Class IV laser therapy.

Hand & Foot Adjustments

Specialized extremity care for wrists, hands, ankles, and feet—including concerns like carpal tunnel symptoms and plantar fasciitis. Learn more about our hand & foot specialties.
